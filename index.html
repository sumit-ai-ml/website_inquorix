Practical questions.

Can I use data I already have?+

Chat with customer supports CSV and Excel (.xlsx) files, plus PNG, JPEG, and WebP images. Image transcriptions require confirmation before calculations. Voice of customer analyzes written answers from completed surveys in your workspace.

Are insights updated automatically?+

Voice-of-customer results are saved snapshots. Run a new analysis to include new responses or change categories. A chat keeps its initial survey snapshot; start a new conversation for fresh survey data.

How do I find the right access for my team?+

Sign in to your workspace, or contact us about workspace access and pricing. AI features and usage limits depend on workspace configuration.
